Nutrition guide

Target a ~700 kcal/day deficit. For a 75kg male roughly 1,800-2,000 kcal/day. Hit 1.6-2.0g protein per kg bodyweight (~120-150g/day) to preserve muscle.

✓ Protein every meal — chicken, fish, eggs, tofu, Greek yogurt
✓ Carbs around workouts (rice, oats, fruit)
✓ Vegetables fill remaining plate
✓ 2-3 liters water daily
✗ Liquid calories (beer, juice) — silent deficit killer
✓ 1 free MEAL per week (not a free day)
What to expect

Week 1-2: Scale drops fast (water + glycogen). Don't get cocky.
Week 3-4: Slower, real fat loss. This is where most people quit. Don't.
Strength: Up by week 2. Track your lifts.
Knee: Should feel notably better by week 3 if you do rehab daily.
V-shape: Becomes visible as fat drops + lats and delts grow.
